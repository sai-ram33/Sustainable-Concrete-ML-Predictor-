Input Parameters

The model uses parameters related to concrete mix design and curing conditions.

Water-Binder Ratio
Represents the ratio of water to total binder and is an important factor influencing concrete strength and durability.
Curing Age
The age of the concrete in days at the time of testing (e.g., 3, 7, 28, or 90 days).
Curing Condition
The environment in which the concrete is cured, such as water immersion or air curing.

Model Limitations

Machine learning predictions are data-driven estimates based on the training dataset. Prediction reliability depends on the quality, quantity, and range of the available data.

Actual concrete performance can also be affected by:

  • Material properties and variability
  • Mixing and batching procedures
  • Placement and compaction techniques
  • Curing environment variations
  • Testing procedures and equipment

Therefore, ML predictions should support preliminary assessment and research, not replace laboratory testing or applicable concrete design standards.

In Simple Terms: The model learns relationships between concrete mix parameters, curing conditions, sustainable material replacement, and measured concrete performance. After the user provides the required design parameters, the system processes the information and generates predicted concrete properties.
